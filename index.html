📚 Learn About Online Scams

Understand common types of cyber fraud

🎣 Phishing

Fraudsters send fake emails, SMS or links pretending to be banks, companies or trusted organizations to steal personal information.

🔐 OTP Fraud

Scammers may pretend to be bank employees and ask for your OTP. Never share your OTP with anyone.

💳 UPI Fraud

Fraudsters may send fake payment requests or convince victims to scan a QR code or enter their UPI PIN.

🏦 KYC Scam

Fake messages may claim that your bank account or SIM will be blocked unless you immediately update KYC.

💼 Fake Job Scam

Scammers offer fake jobs and ask candidates to pay registration, training or security fees.

🎁 Lottery Scam

Victims receive fake messages claiming they won a lottery or prize and must pay a fee to receive it.

📈 Investment Scam

Fraudsters promise guaranteed high returns and pressure victims to transfer money to fake investment platforms.

📱 Social Media Scam

Fake accounts may impersonate friends, celebrities or companies and ask for money or personal information.

☎️ Fake Customer Care

Scammers create fake customer-support numbers and request remote access, OTPs or banking information.

🛡️ Safety Tips

1️⃣ Protect OTP

Never share OTP, PIN or password with anyone.

2️⃣ Verify Links

Check the website address before entering sensitive information.

3️⃣ Avoid Unknown APKs

Do not install applications received from unknown sources.

4️⃣ Verify Calls

Use official websites or apps to find customer-care numbers.

5️⃣ Don't Rush

Scammers often create urgency to make victims act without thinking.

6️⃣ Verify Payment Requests

Never approve a payment request without confirming who sent it.

🚨 Report Cyber Fraud

Use official channels for reporting.

Important: If you have already lost money because of a cyber financial fraud, report it immediately. Do not wait.
